Capabilities & Product Portfolio

Products & Manufacturing Capabilities

Comprehensive composite engineering spanning aerodynamic test models, flight simulator enclosures, radar radomes, parabolic reflectors, HVAC housings, and industrial machinery enclosures.

Documented Product Lines

Specialized Composite Product Lines

Aerodynamic Testing

Wind & Water Tunnel Models

Scaled aircraft, UAV, missile, naval ship, and vehicle models engineered for aerodynamic force measurement and hydrodynamic flow visualization.

Features integrated surface pressure tapping capillaries, precision sting mounts, internal balance fixture pockets, and mirror-smooth gelcoat finishing.

Flight Simulation

Cockpit Simulator & Mock-Up Shells

Full-scale 1:1 environmental simulator cockpits, canopy bubble framing, windscreen arch assemblies, and aircraft engineering mock-up shells.

Fabricated with embedded structural mounting inserts, glare shields, modular floor platforms, and provisions for multi-channel pilot instrumentation displays.

RF & Projection

GFRP Radomes & Spherical Domes

Low-loss RF radomes for blade, monopole, and dipole antennas, honeycomb-sandwiched dorsal covers, and 6m to 9m spherical projection domes for simulators.

Constructed with controlled dielectric glass-resin formulations to ensure RF signal transparency, high weather resistance, and seamless visual projection.

Carbon Fiber Composites

CFRP Products & Reflectors

Precision single-piece and segmented CFRP parabolic reflectors (600mm to 4.6m), Ku-band offset dishes, engine nacelles, and aircraft equipment racks.

Engineered using high-modulus carbon fabric cured under vacuum bag compression, delivering near-zero thermal expansion and high surface contour fidelity.

HVAC & Commercial Fleet

Air Conditioner Housings & Bases

Series production of UV-stabilized, impact-resistant GFRP rooftop air conditioner housing covers, condenser pans, and evaporator bases for commercial coach buses.

Features molded mounting flanges, integrated water drainage channels, weather-sealed joints, and long-term UV resistance under continuous road vibration.

Industrial Engineering

Machine Tool Enclosures & Covers

Custom GFRP enclosures for CNC machinery, gear hobbing centers, transit boxes, and integrated structural metal scaffolding platforms.

Resistant to aggressive coolants, lubricants, and metal chips, providing superior operator acoustic dampening compared to conventional sheet metal.
