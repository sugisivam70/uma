Our Work & Composite Applications

An interactive showcase of illustrative component categories representing UMA Enterprises' documented manufacturing expertise across aerospace, aerodynamic research, defence simulation, and industrial transit.

Illustrative Visual Disclosure: The photographs and visuals displayed below are illustrative manufacturing representations created to demonstrate UMA Enterprises' verified fabrication processes and capabilities. In strict accordance with customer privacy and defence non-disclosure agreements, proprietary engineering drawings, purchase order records, and confidential assembly details are not published.
Aerospace · Illustrative

Aerospace Fairings & Nacelles

Precision-moulded composite fairings, CFRP air intakes, and engine nacelles engineered for low drag and high stiffness.

Constructed via vacuum bag moulding with woven carbon fiber twill and high-temperature epoxy, ensuring high vibrational resistance.

Aerodynamic Research · Illustrative

Scaled Wind Tunnel Test Models

Scaled aerodynamic aircraft and naval test models with integrated pressure tappings for force-balance measurement.

Equipped with internal balance interfaces, calibrated static pressure ports, and smooth gelcoat surfaces to avoid flow boundary disruption.

Simulation · Illustrative

Cockpit Simulator Enclosures

1:1 scale environmental flight simulator cockpits, canopy arch assemblies, and spherical visual projection domes.

Modular composite construction allowing rapid facility assembly, complete with glare shields and instrument mounting points.

Communication · Illustrative

Radomes & CFRP Parabolic Reflectors

Dielectric antenna radomes and multi-segmented carbon fiber parabolic reflectors from 600mm to 4.6m diameter.

Designed for minimal insertion loss across UHF/Ku bands and high surface contour fidelity under outdoor environmental loading.

Industrial Tooling · Illustrative

Machine Tool GFRP Enclosures

Custom chemical and coolant-resistant fiberglass enclosures for CNC machining centers and gear hobbing equipment.

Provides operator safety shielding and acoustic noise dampening while eliminating the corrosion issues common to steel sheet enclosures.

Automotive & HVAC · Illustrative

Transit AC Housings & Vehicle Bonnets

Molded rooftop air conditioner housings and bases for commercial coaches, truck bonnets, and EV battery trays.

Engineered for high UV stability, road vibration dampening, and lightweight aerodynamic efficiency across commercial fleets.

Fiberglass · Illustrative

Mould Tooling & Sandwich Structures

Master pattern tooling, LRTM moulds, and structural composite sandwich panels utilizing PUF and Rohacell cores.

Allows rapid prototyping of one-off components and serves as the master tooling for repeat industrial production.

Custom Engineering · Illustrative

Bespoke Composite Engineering

Specialized composite air splitters, ground support equipment, and bonded metal-composite structural assemblies.

Tailored manufacturing for customer-specific engineering challenges, from CAD review to material test certificate sign-off.
