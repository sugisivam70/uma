Fabrication Methods

Documented Composite Processes

Mastery of five distinct fabrication methodologies tailored to geometry, volume, and mechanical specifications.

All Capabilities

Hand Layup & Contact Moulding

Meticulous manual reinforcement placement and calibrated resin wet-out for complex contours, prototype shells, and architectural composite covers.

Vacuum & Pressure Bag Moulding

Consolidation using high-vacuum systems (ED 15 Hivac) to remove voids, compress laminates, and achieve high strength-to-weight ratios.

Spray-Up Technique (Bullows Gun)

Controlled spray deposition of chopped strand fibers and catalyzed resin for rapid, uniform wall-thickness on large industrial enclosures.

Match Die Moulding

Precision matched tooling producing parts with smooth cosmetic surfaces on both faces and exact dimensional tolerances for repeat assemblies.

PUF & Rohacell FRP Sandwiching

Structural sandwich composites integrating Polyurethane Foam (PUF) or Rohacell cores between rigid composite skins for maximum deflection resistance.

Engineering Advantages

Why Advanced Composites?

Engineered GFRP and CFRP composites replace heavier, corrosion-prone metallic assemblies across mission-critical applications.

Weight Reduction & Specific Strength Up to 60% lighter than steel and 30% lighter than aluminum with superior specific tensile modulus.
Corrosion, Weather & Chemical Immunity Zero oxidation, resistant to harsh UV exposure, coolants, industrial acids, and maritime environments.
Electromagnetic & RF Transparency Custom dielectric properties essential for radar domes, communication antenna radomes, and sensor shrouds.
Manufacturing Workflow

From Concept to Certified Component

A disciplined 5-stage manufacturing methodology ensuring technical compliance and on-time delivery.

View Facility
Phase 01

CAD Geometry & Tooling

Digital design analysis, draft angle verification, master pattern fabrication, and dedicated composite mould preparation.

Phase 02

Reinforcement & Layup

Calibrated plies of woven roving, chopped strand mat, or carbon twill laid with strict fiber orientation control.

Phase 03

Consolidation & Curing

Vacuum bagging or match die compaction to extract entrapped air and cure resins under controlled thermal conditions.

Phase 04

Trimming & Assembly

CNC/manual precision edge trimming, drill jig hole location, metal bracket bonding, and polyurethane coating.

Phase 05

Inspection & Dispatch

Coordinate checks, surface roughness measurement, content test report generation, and secure transit packaging.
