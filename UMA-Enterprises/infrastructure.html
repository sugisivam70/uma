Fabrication Processes

Documented Manufacturing Methods

Proven composite moulding processes deployed strictly according to part geometry, structural load cases, and production volume.

1. Hand Layup / Contact Moulding

Controlled manual placement of E-glass woven roving, chopped strand mat, and carbon plies with calibrated resin wet-out. Ideal for large aerodynamic shells, radomes, and complex prototype mock-ups.

2. Spray-Up Technique (Bullows Gun)

Automated chopping of continuous glass rovings combined with catalyzed resin spray via Bullows equipment, achieving uniform thickness and structural consistency across commercial transit AC covers.

3. Vacuum Bagging (ED 15 Hivac)

Atmospheric compaction using our dedicated ED 15 Hivac machine. Evacuates entrapped air voids and excess resin, optimizing fiber-volume fractions for aerospace structural panels and parabolic reflectors.

4. Match Die Moulding

Closed two-part male/female matched tooling providing smooth, cosmetic gelcoat surfaces on both sides with tight wall-thickness tolerances, essential for repeat automotive panels and instrument covers.

5. PUF & Rohacell FRP Sandwiching

High-stiffness, ultralight structural panels sandwiching rigid Polyurethane Foam (PUF) or Rohacell polymethacrylimide cores between GFRP or CFRP skins, engineered for radar anechoic chambers and transit floors.

Quality Philosophy

ISO 9001:2015 Aligned Quality System

At UMA Enterprises, quality is not a standalone check at the end of the line—it is embedded into every operational step from raw resin testing to final component sign-off.

Metrology & Inspection

In-Process Inspection & Finishing

Critical milestones include checking mould surface roughness before gelcoat application, monitoring resin gelation times, verifying vacuum seal integrity throughout the cure cycle, and inspecting edge trimming with specialized templates.

Material Content Test Reports

Every supplied batch of composite products can be delivered with formal test reports detailing fiber-to-resin ratio (burn-off tests), Barcol hardness, and dimensional deviation reports to satisfy incoming quality protocols.

Continuous Process Refinement

We continuously review quality metrics with client engineers and certified chemical suppliers, refining layup patterns, resin rheology, and release agents to eliminate porosity and maximize component longevity.
