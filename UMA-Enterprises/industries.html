Sector-Specific Engineering

Industries We Serve

From supersonic aircraft wind tunnel models and military simulator shells to transit bus rooftop air conditioning housings and Ku-band carbon reflectors, discover our documented composite applications.

01 · Aerospace & Aviation

Aerodynamic Testing, Tooling & Flight Shells

UMA Enterprises supports national aerospace programs, aircraft divisions, and commercial aviation innovators with high-accuracy scaled aerodynamic models, vacuum-formed windscreen canopy tooling, annealing fixtures, and full-scale aircraft mock-ups.

• Scaled Wind Tunnel Models: High-precision force-measurement models with integrated static pressure tappings.
• Canopy Bubble & Trimming Tools: Specialized blow-forming dies and trimming fixtures for cockpit transparencies.
• Aircraft Mock-Up Shells: 1:1 scale engineering and display mock-ups with internal structural mounting provisions.

02 · Defence & Research

Flight Simulator Cockpits, UAVs & Test Rigs

Serving defence research and development establishments with 1:1 scale environmental flight simulator cockpits, computer-based pilot selection shells, tactical UAV airframe mock-ups, and anechoic chamber test structures.

• Environmental Simulator Cockpits: Ergonomic pilot cockpit enclosures fitted with modular console mountings.
• Tactical UAV & Missile Mock-Ups: Full-scale airframe replicas, engine nacelles, and telemetry antenna masts.
• Anechoic Chamber Structures: 15ft GFRP Rohacell-sandwiched cylinders for electromagnetic signature testing.

03 · Automotive & Transportation

Commercial Vehicle Bonnets & EV Casings

Lightweight FRP bonnet kit assemblies for heavy commercial trucks, electric vehicle battery protective enclosures, and full-scale automotive aerodynamic evaluation models.

04 · HVAC & Refrigeration

Transit Rooftop AC Housings & Bases

Series manufacturing of UV-stabilized, impact-resistant GFRP housing covers and base trays for bus rooftop air conditioners, evaporator shrouds, and condenser units.

05 · Industrial Engineering

Machine Enclosures & Heavy Tooling

Heavy-duty corrosion-proof GFRP enclosures for CNC milling and gear hobbing machinery, durable transit boxes, and integrated composite-metal scaffolding structures.

06 · Renewable Energy

Wind Turbine Blades & Master Moulds

Master tooling and prototype fabrication for 10kW wind turbine blade moulds, scaled wind wheel blades with pressure tappings, and aerodynamic composite airfoils.

07 · Communication & Antenna Systems

CFRP Parabolic Reflectors & Radomes

High-accuracy composite antenna hardware covering single-segment and multi-segmented CFRP parabolic reflectors (600mm to 4.6m), Ku-band offset dishes, and dielectric GFRP radomes for ground stations and mobile satellite terminals.

• Segmented Carbon Reflectors: High surface contour accuracy with near-zero thermal distortion for satellite tracking.
• Low-Loss Antenna Radomes: Controlled wall thickness optimizing RF transmission across C, S, and UHF bands.
