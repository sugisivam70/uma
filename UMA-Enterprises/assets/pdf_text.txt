                                                            PROFILE 
 
We take the liberty of introducing ourselves as fabricators of Fiber Glass Reinforced 
Products, having specialized in this field since 2001 and with a short span of decade’s 
time we have positioned ourselves in this competitive arena by giving priority to quality 
and fabrication of composite products above everything else. Commitment to Customer 
satisfaction is achieved by pursuit of excellence in all aspects with high technical skills, 
communication and teamwork. This has helped us to reduce delay in delivery and provide 
high degree of flexibility in meeting customer demands on time. 
 
 
                                          MANUFACTURING FACILITY   
 
We have a full-fledged fabrication facility for GFRP products at Anagalapura village , 
Dodda Gubbi post , Bidarahalli hobli which is 14 kms from the heart of Bangalore city 
with factory space area of 8000 Sq Ft. 
 
 
                                                    PERFORMANCE 
 
We at UMA ENTERPRISES , an ISO 9001 2015 partnership firm concern believe that 
performance is dedicated teamwork where people coordinate at all levels to achieve 
common goals. We strive for perfection and improvement of methods of fabrication  
through a process of continuous partnership with our customers and suppliers. Consistent 
quality products are rendered in consultation with our Customers who constantly review 
quality issues.
