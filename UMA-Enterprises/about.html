Company Profile · Est. 2001

Built on Composite Craftsmanship & Engineering Discipline.

Established in 2001, UMA Enterprises is a specialized partnership firm dedicated to Fiber Glass Reinforced Plastic (GFRP) and advanced CFRP composite fabrication for demanding aerospace, defence, and industrial applications.

Our Philosophy

Customer Satisfaction Through Technical Mastery

At UMA Enterprises, we believe performance is dedicated teamwork where people coordinate at all levels to achieve common engineering goals.

Quality Assurance

Company Journey Since 2001

Founded in 2001 in Bengaluru, UMA Enterprises established itself in the competitive composite sector by giving unconditional priority to material quality and meticulous fabrication methods. Within a decade of operation, we expanded from standard industrial fiberglass products into tight-tolerance wind tunnel test models, 1:1 scale aircraft mock-up shells, and flight simulator cockpit structures.

Customer Collaboration & Continuous Improvement

We strive for continuous improvement of fabrication methods through a process of active partnership with our customers and certified raw material suppliers. Consistent, high-performance composite products are rendered through ongoing consultation with client engineering teams who review dimensional, electrical, and structural requirements at every critical stage.

8,000 sq. ft. Integrated Manufacturing Facility

Our manufacturing plant provides an 8,000 sq. ft. dedicated shop floor located in Bengaluru, equipped with dedicated bays for master pattern development, moulding, resin lamination, vacuum bagging consolidation, trimming, metal assembly, and final quality inspection.

Engineering Support & Turnaround Speed

High technical competence and transparent communication across all operational units have enabled us to minimize production delays and offer exceptional flexibility, accommodating tight delivery schedules and dynamic client design revisions without compromising structural integrity.

Mission, Vision & Values

Guiding Principles of Our Craft

Our Mission

To fabricate dimensionally accurate, structurally robust GFRP and CFRP composite products by applying disciplined process controls, utilizing certified materials, and maintaining an uncompromised commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be recognized as India's premier, technically versatile composite manufacturing partner for aerospace test programs, defence simulation structures, renewable energy components, and custom industrial engineering.

Collaborative Partnership

Integrity & Confidentiality

Strict safeguarding of customer proprietary drawings, specifications, and project scope requirements.

Two Decades of Evolution

Timeline of Growth & Milestones

From foundational fiberglass components in 2001 to advanced aerodynamic test models, flight simulator shells, and satellite reflectors.

2001 — Establishment

Founding of UMA Enterprises

Established as a specialized partnership firm in Bengaluru dedicated to Fiber Glass Reinforced Plastic (GFRP) component fabrication for regional engineering industries.

2005–2010 — Aerospace Expansion

Wind Tunnel Models & Flight Simulators

Pioneered precision scaled wind tunnel and water tunnel test models with integrated pressure tappings, along with 1:1 scale environmental flight simulator cockpits for leading aerospace establishments.

2011–2018 — Large-Scale Composites

CFRP Reflectors, Radomes & Transit HVAC

Expanded capabilities to segmented carbon fiber parabolic reflectors (up to 4.6m), large-span simulator domes (up to 9m), and series production of bus rooftop AC housings.

2019–Present — Certified Operations

8,000 sq. ft. Facility & ISO 9001:2015

Consolidated operations at an 8,000 sq. ft. modern facility in Bengaluru with ISO 9001:2015 quality management alignment (subject to current verification), UDYAM MSME registration, SAMAR, and ZED Bronze credentials.

Quality Credentials

Certifications & Registration Standards

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management System standard governing raw material inspection, resin mixing ratios, mould maintenance, and final dispatch test reports (subject to current certification verification).

UDYAM MSME & SAMAR

Registered MSME enterprise holding SAMAR and ZED Bronze certification recognition, supporting domestic defense indigenization and high-reliability industrial manufacturing.

Traceable Test Documentation

Every supplied composite shipment is accompanied by test reports detailing resin/fiber contents, cure cycle parameters, and dimensional conformity as agreed with the client.
